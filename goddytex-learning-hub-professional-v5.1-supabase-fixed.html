Eze Godwin is an agriculture and animal-health professional with hands-on experience in farm management, animal production and health management. He also develops skills in data analysis and digital marketing, bringing practical business and technology ideas into learning.

The goal of Goddytex Learning Hub is to make useful skills accessible to beginners through clear lessons, practical exercises and real-world projects. Whether you are learning data analysis, digital marketing, agriculture or animal health, the focus is on understanding the subject and knowing how to apply it.
